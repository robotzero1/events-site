Motril Book Fair

On now · until Monday 2 November

Twenty bookshops and publishers from the province fill the arcades of the Plaza de España for five days, with new books, second-hand stalls and a corner for children.

There are readings by local writers each evening and a storytelling hour for children on Saturday morning. Bring a bag: most stalls take cards, a few only cash.

More information on the town hall's website
